## 3. ACADEMIC EVALUATION, ATTENDANCE & PASSING ORDINANCES

### 3.1 Attendance Requirements & Vice-Chancellor Condonation
1. **Mandatory Minimum Attendance:** A student must secure a minimum of **75%** attendance in aggregate across theory lectures, tutorials, and practical laboratories to be eligible to appear in end-semester examinations.
2. **Vice-Chancellor Condonation:** The Vice-Chancellor may grant attendance condonation up to a maximum of **10%** (reducing the required minimum attendance threshold to **65%**) exclusively on documented medical emergencies or official university representation.
3. **Debarment Threshold:** Any student having less than **65%** attendance is strictly debarred from appearing in end-semester examinations.

### 3.2 Dual-Threshold Passing Criteria (CIA & ESE)
Under JRU Academic Ordinance Clause 5.2, every course evaluates students via a dual-threshold passing criterion:
- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA):** 40% weightage. Students must secure at least **40%** in CIA to pass.
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE):** 60% weightage. Students must secure at least **40%** in ESE to pass.
- A student must independently satisfy both thresholds (**40%** in CIA and **40%** in ESE). Failing either component results in Grade F.

### 3.3 ICAR Compliance for Agriculture Programs
- For students enrolled in **B.Sc (Honours) Agriculture**, the passing criterion adheres strictly to Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) 5th Deans Committee standards, requiring a minimum of **50%** aggregate marks to pass each academic course and examination.

## 4. ADMISSIONS ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE):** The minimum eligibility requirement is passing 10+2 (Higher Secondary) with Physics, Mathematics, and Chemistry/Computer Science with at least **50%** aggregate marks (45% for reserved SC/ST/OBC categories).
- **Lateral Entry B.Tech:** Polytechnic Diploma in Engineering with at least 50% marks.

1. **Free Rural Legal Aid Clinic:**
   - **Yes**, Jharkhand Rai University's Faculty of Legal Studies operates an active **Legal Aid** Clinic that provides free legal counseling, pro-bono advice, and mediation services to neighboring rural communities in Kamre, Ratu, and Ranchi district.
2. **Defense & Paramilitary Concession:**
   - Children and wards of Indian Armed Forces and Paramilitary personnel (Army, Navy, Air Force, CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, and Police) receive a direct **20%** tuition fee concession under the Veer Shaheed scholarship scheme.
