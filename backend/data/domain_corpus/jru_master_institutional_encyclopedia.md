## 1. UNIVERSITY GOVERNANCE & STATUTORY FRAMEWORK

### 1.1 Legislative Foundation & Territorial Jurisdiction
Jharkhand Rai University was established under the Jharkhand Rai University Act, 2011 (Jharkhand Act No. 03 of 2012) passed by the Legislative Assembly of Jharkhand. The University is vested with the authority to confer Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral (Ph.D.) degrees across Science, Engineering, Information Technology, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Management, Commerce, Legal Studies, and Agriculture.

### 1.2 Statutory Authorities of the University
1. **The Governing Body:** The supreme authority responsible for university policy formulation, asset management, and general superintendence.
2. **The Board of Management:** Oversees executive administration, appointment of academic staff, infrastructure development, and budget approvals.
3. **The Academic Council:** The apex academic regulatory body responsible for curriculum approval, standard of instruction, examination ordinances, research guidelines, and academic collaboration.
4. **The Board of Studies (BOS):** Departmental academic committees that periodically revise and update course syllabi in alignment with AICTE, PCI, BCI, and UGC model curricula.
5. **The Finance Committee:** Advises the Board of Management on all matters of annual revenue, capital expenditure, student fee structures, and audited accounts.

## 2. ACADEMIC ORDINANCES & REGULATIONS

### 2.1 Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) Architecture
The University operates strictly under the UGC Choice Based Credit System (CBCS). Academic programs are structured into:
- **Core Courses (CC):** Compulsory discipline-specific foundational courses.
- **Discipline Specific Electives (DSE):** Advanced specialized subjects chosen by students within their parent discipline.
- **Generic Electives (GE):** Interdisciplinary courses offered by other departments to broaden technical and humanitarian literacy.
- **Ability Enhancement Compulsory Courses (AECC):** English communication, environmental science, and technical report writing.
- **Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC):** Industry-focused hands-on certifications, cloud labs, and coding bootcamps.

### 2.2 Attendance Regulations (Ordinance Clause 4.2)
1. **Mandatory Minimum Attendance:** Every registered student must achieve a minimum of **75% attendance** across lecture hours, tutorials, and laboratory practical sessions.
2. **Condonation of Shortage:** 
   - The Vice-Chancellor is empowered to condone attendance shortages up to a maximum of **10%** (reducing the threshold to **65%**) under extraordinary documented circumstances:
     * Acute illness requiring hospitalization, supported by a government medical officer / registered civil surgeon certificate.
     * Official representation of the University in National / State Level sports, cultural, NCC, or NSS events.
   - Medical documents must be submitted to the Dean's Office within **7 working days** of the student resuming classes.
3. **Debarment:** Students securing less than **65% aggregate attendance** are strictly debarred from appearing in the End-Semester Examination (ESE) for that semester and must re-register for the course when offered next.

### 2.3 Examination & Evaluation Ordinance (Clause 5.1 - 5.8)
The evaluation system is continuous and comprehensive, distributed as:
- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA): 40% Weightage**
  * Mid-Term Examination: 20 Marks
  * Class Quizzes & Assignments: 10 Marks
  * Classroom Attendance & Behavioral Conduct: 10 Marks
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE): 60% Weightage**
  * Comprehensive written/lab examination of 3 hours duration conducted centrally by the Controller of Examinations (COE).
- **Dual-Threshold Passing Criterion:**
  * To clear a paper, a student must secure a minimum of **40% in CIA (16 / 40)** AND a minimum of **40% in ESE (24 / 60)** independently. Failure to achieve 40% in either component results in a letter grade **F (Fail)**.

### 2.5 SGPA and CGPA Mathematical Computations
1. **Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):**
   $$\text{SGPA} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} (C_i \times G_i)}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} C_i}$$
   where $C_i$ is the number of credits for course $i$, and $G_i$ is the grade point earned.

2. **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):**
   $$\text{CGPA} = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^{M} (\text{SGPA}_j \times S_j)}{\sum_{j=1}^{M} S_j}$$
   where $S_j$ is the total credit count of semester $j$.

3. **Official CGPA-to-Percentage Conversion:**
   Under approved Academic Council Notification, equivalent percentage is computed strictly via:
   $$\text{Equivalent Percentage (\%)} = (\text{CGPA} - 0.75) \times 10$$

### 2.6 Academic Backlog, Promotion & Year-Back Rules (Ordinance Clause 6.4)
- **Maximum Permissible Backlogs for Promotion:** A student carrying no more than **4 back-papers** across the preceding academic semesters is provisionally promoted to the next academic year.
- **Year-Back Condition:** A student carrying **5 or more uncleared back-papers** is placed on Year-Back status. They cannot register for advanced courses until backlogs are reduced below the threshold.
- **Supplementary Examination Cycle:** Supplementary examinations are held twice annually — in July/August for even semesters, and in December/January for odd semesters.
