## 3. INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE PROGRAMS

Jharkhand Rai University commits over ₹1.5 Crores annually in student financial aid to promote equity, merit, and regional inclusion:

### 3.1 Merit-Cum-Means University Scholarship Matrix
Applicable on tuition fees for the entire program duration subject to maintaining a minimum CGPA of 7.5 with no backlogs:

- **Category A (95.00% and above in Qualifying Examination):** **100% Tuition Fee Waiver**
- **Category B (85.00% to 94.99% in Qualifying Examination):** **50% Tuition Fee Waiver**
- **Category C (75.00% to 84.99% in Qualifying Examination):** **25% Tuition Fee Waiver**

### 3.2 Affirmative Action & Social Inclusion Concessions
1. **"Beti Padhao, Desh Badhao" Scholarship:**
   - A direct **15% concession on tuition fees** awarded to all female candidates admitted into Engineering, Mining, and Agriculture to promote women in STEM.
2. **Jharkhand State E-Kalyan Welfare Scheme:**
   - Complete tuition fee reimbursement processed directly through the Department of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, Minority and Backward Class Welfare, Government of Jharkhand, for eligible domicile students under state norms.
3. **Wards of Defense & Paramilitary Personnel:**
   - **20% tuition concession** for children of Indian Armed Forces, CRPF, CISF, and Jharkhand Police personnel.
4. **Siblings Concession:**
   - **10% fee reduction** for younger siblings enrolled concurrently at JRU.

## 5. CAMPUS LIFE, CLUBS & STUDENT SUPPORT DIRECTORY

### 5.1 Student Clubs & Extracurricular Activities
1. **Robo-Tech & Coding Club:** Organizes 24-hour hackathons, AI workshops, and competitive programming challenges.
2. **Cultural & Literary Society ("Rhythm"):** Hosts the annual university mega-fest *"Udaan"*, poetry slams, and theatrical drama productions.
3. **Sports & Athletic Council:** Organizes the inter-collegiate sports championship *"Spardha"* featuring cricket, football, volleyball, badminton, and table tennis.
4. **Legal Aid Clinic:** Managed by the Faculty of Legal Studies, providing free legal literacy camps and counseling to neighboring rural communities in Ratu and Namkum.
5. **NSS & Red Ribbon Club:** Community blood donation drives, environmental tree plantation, and digital literacy camps in adopted village clusters.

### 5.2 Mandatory Statutory Compliance & Anti-Ragging Directives
- **Zero Tolerance Policy:** Ragging in any form (verbal, physical, or psychological) inside or outside the campus is strictly prohibited under the UGC Regulations on Curbing the Menace of Ragging in Higher Educational Institutions, 2009.
- **Punitive Action:** Proven cases attract immediate FIR registration, rustication, and cancellation of admission.
- **National Anti-Ragging Toll-Free Helpline:** `1800-180-5522`
