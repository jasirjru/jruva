# Jharkhand Rai University (JRU) — Academic Handbook & Official Regulations

## 1. Institutional Overview & Vision
Jharkhand Rai University (JRU), established in Ranchi, Jharkhand, is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, and accredited by NAAC. The university's mission is to provide cutting-edge, accessible technical, management, and pharmaceutical education with an emphasis on research, industry readiness, and ethical leadership.

Campus Location: Kamre, Ratu Road, Ranchi, Jharkhand - 835222.

## 2. Academic Programs Offered

### 2.1 Faculty of Science and Engineering
- **Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**: 4-Year (8 Semesters) undergraduate engineering program. Specialization tracks include Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI/ML), Cloud Computing, and Cyber Security.
- **B.Tech Lateral Entry**: 3-Year (6 Semesters) program for Diploma holders in Engineering or B.Sc graduates.
- **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)**: 3-Year (6 Semesters) professional computing program.
- **Master of Computer Applications (MCA)**: 2-Year (4 Semesters) postgraduate software engineering program.
- **Diploma in Engineering**: 3-Year polytechnic diploma in CSE, Mechanical, Mining, and Civil Engineering.

### 2.2 Faculty of Management & Commerce
- **Master of Business Administration (MBA)**: 2-Year dual specialization (Finance, Marketing, Human Resource Management, Information Technology).
- **Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)**: 3-Year undergraduate management program.

### 2.3 Faculty of Pharmacy
- **Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm)**: 4-Year PCI-approved professional pharmacy degree.
- **Diploma in Pharmacy (D.Pharm)**: 2-Year PCI-approved diploma.

## 3. Academic Evaluation & 10-Point Grading System

JRU adheres to the UGC Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) utilizing a 10-point grading scale for SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) and CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) calculation:

| Percentage Range (%) | Grade Letter | Grade Point | Performance Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90.00 – 100.00** | **O** | **10.0** | Outstanding |
| **80.00 – 89.99** | **A+** | **9.0** | Excellent |
| **70.00 – 79.99** | **A** | **8.0** | Very Good |
| **60.00 – 69.99** | **B+** | **7.0** | Good |
| **50.00 – 59.99** | **B** | **6.0** | Above Average |
| **45.00 – 49.99** | **C** | **5.0** | Average |
| **40.00 – 44.99** | **P** | **4.0** | Pass |
| **Below 40.00** | **F** | **0.0** | Fail / Re-appear |
| **Absent** | **Ab** | **0.0** | Absent |

### 3.1 Evaluation Weightage
- **Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA)**: 40% of total course marks (Mid-semester tests: 20%, Assignments/Quizzes: 10%, Attendance & Class Participation: 10%).
- **End-Semester Examination (ESE)**: 60% of total course marks (Conducted centrally by the Controller of Examinations).

### 3.2 Passing Criteria & Promotion
- To pass a course, a student must secure a minimum of 40% marks in both CIA and ESE independently, with an aggregate grade point of at least 4.0 (Grade P).
- **Minimum Attendance Rule**: A minimum of **75% attendance** across all theory and laboratory sessions is mandatory to be eligible to sit for the End-Semester Examination. Condonation up to 10% may be granted by the Vice-Chancellor on documented medical emergencies or official university representation.
- **Year Promotion**: A student is promoted to the subsequent academic year provided they do not carry more than 4 back-papers across the preceding two semesters.

## 6. Scholarships & Financial Assistance

1. **Merit Scholarship**:
   - **50% Tuition Fee Waiver**: For candidates scoring $\ge 85\%$ aggregate marks in the qualifying 10+2 / graduation examination.
   - **25% Tuition Fee Waiver**: For candidates scoring between $75.00\% - 84.99\%$.
2. **State Welfare (e-Kalyan) Scholarship**:
   - Available to SC, ST, and OBC domicile students of Jharkhand under the Department of Welfare, Government of Jharkhand.
3. **Sports & Cultural Distinction Waiver**:
   - Up to 100% waiver on sports quota for state/national level award-winning athletes.
